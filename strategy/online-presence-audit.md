## What Gemini says when asked "production x" — two real findings

**1. It's citing a location that was deliberately removed.** Gemini says
ProductionX serves "Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam" — but the git history shows
Vizag and Pan-India claims were intentionally dropped from the site. This
means stale information is still circulating in AI answers, likely from an
old cached version or an external directory listing that still says Vizag.
Worth a search for where that's still published and correcting it at the
source — an AI giving out a wrong service area is a real credibility risk if
a prospect asks about it directly.

**2. When asked "is it trusted," Gemini's own advice is: ask for case
studies.** External, independent confirmation of the exact gap already
flagged — proof is the thing missing, and it's the first thing a cautious
buyer is told to go check.

**3. Naming disambiguation problem.** "Production X" competes for attention
against unrelated entities (defence manufacturing, "Make in India" results).
Not a fast fix, but it means content and structured data should keep pairing
the name with disambiguating context — Hyderabad, brand studio, the three
disciplines — rather than assuming "ProductionX" alone is enough to be
recognised.
