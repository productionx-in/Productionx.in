# ProductionX — The 100-Day Content Bible

**Print this. Pin it up. Check it every Monday.**

This is the operating structure for every platform, every week, day 1 to day
100. Full scripts for the week you're executing live in `content/week-NN.md`.
This document is the map — what happens when, why, and how to measure it.

One honest rule this whole bible follows: **exact scripts get written one
month at a time, using what actually worked the month before.** Writing word
-for-word captions for week 12 today would mean guessing twice — once now,
and again when it turns out wrong. The calendar below has zero gaps. The
scripts get filled in as each month starts, from a formula proven to work.

## 2. Who we're actually talking to

Three buyers, not a generic audience. Every piece of content should be
written for one of these three people specifically.

### Persona A — The Developer/Founder (the OTHO shape)
Building something from scratch — a project, a launch, a brand — and needs
strategy, production and a platform together, not piecemeal. Big-ticket,
long sales cycle, decision made personally by the founder. Trusts proof of
end-to-end capability over price. **Reached on:** LinkedIn, YouTube (deep
trust content), referral, direct outreach.

### Persona B — The Premium Local Owner (the Designerz Hub / Utsav shape)
Runs a furniture, fashion, F&B or hospitality business. ₹50k–1.5L/month
budget range. Found via Instagram or local search. Wants to see visible
results — footfall, enquiries, a fuller room — not brand theory. Price-
sensitive but not cheap; needs a real entry rung, not a discount.
**Reached on:** Instagram, Google Business Profile, Facebook groups.

### Persona C — The Brand-Side Marketing Head (the Silver Star / dealership shape)
Works inside a bigger organisation — automotive, retail chain, established
brand. Evaluates vendors formally, cares about process, reliability and
reporting over flash. Longer sales cycle, but higher trust once earned.
**Reached on:** LinkedIn, referral, direct credibility (the founder's
brand-side record speaks directly to this person).

## 4. The 100-day arc — 14 weekly themes

Every week has one theme. All platforms that week orbit it from a different
angle instead of scattering unrelated ideas — easier to plan, and it
reinforces one message instead of eight fragments. Pulls from the 31 ideas in
`strategy/content-topic-bank.md`, repurposed and expanded through the week
(one blog post or case study naturally produces 3–4 social angles).

| 7 | Vertical spotlight: F&B & hospitality | Instagram's existing strength — build on what's already working |
| 8 | Objection-handling — price, trust, timeline | The exact three reasons deals have been lost — answer them publicly, "They Ask You Answer" style |

### Phase 3 — CONVERT & leads (Weeks 9–12, Days 61–90)

| Week | Theme | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | Teardown series — local business audits | Direct lead mechanic: "DM AUDIT," the format that already produced one real lead |

| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | Reviews & referrals, pushed directly | Convert delivered work into GBP reviews and referral asks — closes the RACE loop |

**Scripts for weeks 5–14 get written in month-sized batches** — the full
Month 2 pack (weeks 5–8) gets built in the last few days of Month 1, using
whichever week-1–4 posts actually got saves, comments and DMs as the model.
Same for Month 3. This isn't a delay — it's the difference between guessing
what works and knowing.

numbers before day 30 are noise; whether every planned post actually shipped

## 6. Ad budget — honest, staged, not invented

**Right now: ₹0.** The original strategy called paid media "deliberately
last" for a reason that hasn't changed — there's no cash to fund it, and
unproven organic content is the wrong thing to pay to amplify anyway. Every
number below is a trigger-based plan, not a commitment starting today.

| Stage | Trigger | Budget | What it buys |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Now | — | ₹0 | Organic only, every channel above |
| Test phase | First retainer's first full month of payment received | ₹5,000–8,000/month | 100% spent boosting that month's single best-performing organic post — never a cold campaign. Cheapest way to learn what paid can do, using proof that's already working. |
| Scale phase | Boosted-post data shows a real, positive cost-per-lead | 5–10% of that month's revenue | Split across the platform the test phase proved out, expanded gradually — never jumped to all at once |

No ad spend gets committed without you seeing the actual number and the
actual post it's tied to first — same rule as everything else in this
folder that touches money.

## 7. How to use this document day to day

1. **Monday:** check this week's theme against the calendar in section 4.
2. **Open the matching `content/week-NN.md`** for the exact scripts — write it if the week hasn't been scripted yet (should happen a few days before each new month starts).
3. **Post per the cadence in section 3.** Instagram and LinkedIn are the two to protect if a day gets eaten by client work — see `strategy/daily-operating-schedule.md`.
4. **End of each month:** fill in the actuals row against section 5's targets. What beat the target keeps happening; what missed gets a theory, not a shrug.
5. **Last week of each month:** the next month's scripts get written, built from that month's actual winners.

Nothing in the 100 days is unplanned. What isn't written yet is scheduled to
be written, on a date, from evidence — not left to chance.
