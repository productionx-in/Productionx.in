# ProductionX — profile copy

Ready to paste. Character counts are verified against each platform's limit.

**Two rules that matter more than the words:**

1. **Keep the name, address and phone identical everywhere** — `ProductionX`,
   `Hyderabad, India`, `+91 93919 26846`. Google builds its picture of a
   business by matching those three strings across the web. A "Production X"
   here and a "ProductionX Creative Studio" there splits the entity in two and
   weakens both.
2. **Say the same thing in the same words.** The three disciplines — brand and
   marketing, content production, digital and AI — should read the same on every
   profile. Repetition across platforms is what makes it stick.

---

## A note on how the clients are named

The published roster mixes two different relationships: some are studio
engagements, and some — Mercedes-Benz, Ujwala Group, 1UJ — are where Kiran
worked **brand-side**, as Content Producer and as Head of Creative & Marketing.

The copy below keeps those separate on purpose: brand-side experience is stated
as experience, and studio work is stated as work. That is both more defensible
and the stronger claim — having sat inside the brand beats having invoiced it.
Adjust if any of these were in fact studio engagements.

## After you paste

- **Google Business Profile matters most for enquiries.** It feeds Maps and the
  local pack, which is where "video production near me" actually lands. Fill the
  services list out completely — each entry is separately searchable.
- **Link every profile to `productionx.in`, and link the site back.** The site
  already lists Instagram in its structured data; add the others as you create
  them and they reinforce each other as one entity.
- **Post something within a week of updating.** A profile that changes and then
  goes quiet reads as abandoned to both people and ranking systems.
