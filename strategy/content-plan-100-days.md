# Content plan — Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google — to first revenue by day 100

**The model:** content doesn't earn money. It earns *attention* and *trust*, which
turns into a DM or a call, which turns into a signed client, which earns money.
Every content decision below is judged against that chain — reach that doesn't
lead somewhere is wasted effort, however good the view count looks.

**The honesty check:** Instagram and Google Business Profile can plausibly
produce a client by day 100, because they're fast to reach real intent.
YouTube and blog SEO are compounding assets — they get *stronger* every month
they exist, but Google typically takes 3–6 months to rank new pages and videos
properly. Starting them now is right. Expecting them to pay rent by day 100
is not. Waiting to start them only pushes that payoff further out.

---

## The funnel, in one line per stage

| Stage | Job | Content type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reach** | Get seen by someone who's never heard of you | Reels, Shorts, carousels |
| **Trust** | Prove you can actually do the work | Case studies, process, teardowns, POV |
| **Lead** | Convert a viewer into a conversation | Clear CTA, DM prompts, forms |
| **Client** | Close what the content started | This part is sales, not content — see the OTHO/Designerz plans already in this folder |

Content that only hits "Reach" is a hobby. Every post needs at least a trust or
a lead job to do, or it doesn't go on the calendar.

---

## Instagram — the primary channel, fastest to a lead

Furniture, real estate, hospitality, fashion — every current and target client
is visual and local. This is where the audience already is, and it's the
channel most likely to produce a DM inside 6–8 weeks if posted consistently.

**Cadence: 4–5 posts/week**, batch-recorded so daily filming isn't required.

| Format | What | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reels — process/BTS** | You building something on camera: the Mahati site, a brand system, an AI workflow | Reels get shown to non-followers — this is your reach engine, and "how it's actually made" is what a business owner can't get from a stock template |
| **Reels — before/after** | Transformation cuts: old site vs new, generic branding vs the redo | Fastest-understood proof of skill; highest share rate of any format |
| **Carousels — teardowns** | "3 things this Hyderabad furniture brand's Instagram gets wrong" | Educates + positions expertise + gets saved, which Instagram rewards with more reach |
| **Talking-head Stories/Reels** | You, on camera, giving an opinion or answering a question | This is the trust-builder — a face and a voice is what turns a viewer into a DM |
| **Direct-CTA post, 1×/week** | "DM 'AUDIT' and I'll tear down your current site/profile for free" | This is the piece most people skip. Without an explicit ask, reach doesn't become leads — it just becomes a bigger audience of people who never contact you |

---

## Facebook — repurpose, don't reproduce, plus groups

Facebook Page organic reach is close to zero in 2026 — do not build a separate
content line for it. Two jobs only:

1. **Cross-post everything from Instagram.** Zero extra time cost.
2. **Show up in 3–4 local Hyderabad business/entrepreneur groups**, 2–3
   times/week, answering real questions with real expertise — not dropping
   links. This is where actual business owners (your buyer, not an influencer
   audience) still spend time, and it's a closer demographic match to
   Designerz Hub or OTHO's founder than Instagram's audience is.

---

## YouTube — the compounding trust asset, slower by design

This is not where day-30 leads come from. It's where a prospect goes to
verify you're real before they sign — and where you'll be sending cold-email
recipients and Designerz-Hub-style prospects a link instead of a PDF a year
from now.

**Cadence: 1 long-form video every 2 weeks, Shorts weekly (repurposed Reels — near zero extra cost).**

| Format | What | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Case-study breakdown, 8–12 min** | "How I built [client]'s platform in 6 weeks using AI" | Highest-trust format that exists; ranks in both YouTube and Google search for months to years after publishing, unlike a post that dies in days |
| **Shorts** | Repurposed Instagram Reels | Pure distribution, no new production cost |

Every video published now is a video that's already ranking by month 4–6,
instead of one that hasn't been started yet.

---

## Google / your own site — highest intent, also slower to rank

Someone searching "ad film makers in Hyderabad" or "real estate marketing
agency Hyderabad" is closer to hiring than someone scrolling Instagram. Lower
volume, much higher conversion when it lands — but new pages take months to
rank, so treat this as parallel groundwork, not a near-term source.

**Cadence: 1 blog post/week, Google Business Profile 1 post/week + a review ask after every delivery.**

- **Blog, targeted at buyer search terms** already named in the main strategy
  doc: *ad film makers Hyderabad*, *real estate walkthrough video Hyderabad*,
  *website cost for a furniture brand*. Bottom-of-funnel, high-intent language.
- **Case study pages** for Aruna/Mahati now, OTHO once signed — these double as
  the page a prospect reads right before deciding, and rank for "[industry]
  website design Hyderabad."
- **Google Business Profile is the fastest Google-side win.** It's the one
  piece of this whole plan that can produce a lead inside weeks, not months —
  feeds Maps and the local pack directly. `brand/social-profiles.md` already
  has the copy written; it just needs publishing.
- **FAQ/AI-SEO** (llms.txt already in place) — when someone asks ChatGPT or
  Perplexity "good marketing studio in Hyderabad for real estate," the
  business with structured, quotable answers is the one that gets named. This
  is exactly the channel that already produced one lead.

## The 100-day arc, honestly

**Days 1–30 — foundation, no revenue expected from this yet.**
Post consistently on Instagram, publish 4 blog posts, 2 YouTube videos,
optimise Google Business Profile, start showing up in FB groups. Revenue in
this window comes from the pipeline (OTHO, Mahati, prospecting) — not from
content that's three weeks old.

**Days 30–60 — first signals.**
If Instagram's posted consistently with a real CTA, this is the window where
the first DM-that-isn't-a-friend shows up. Google Business Profile may
produce a local lead here too — it's the fastest-ranking piece of the whole
plan. Blog and YouTube are still mostly building.

**Days 60–100 — plausible first content-sourced client.**
Instagram and Google Business Profile are the realistic sources of an actual
signed client by day 100. Blog posts should be starting to rank for
long-tail terms. YouTube views are growing but this channel's real payoff
window is months 4–6 onward, not day 100.

**What day 100 should actually look like:** a small, real trickle of inbound
from Instagram and local search — not a flood, not a replacement for direct
sales, but proof the engine works and a second lead source next to referral.

## Fitting this inside the day

This runs inside the content block already set in `daily-operating-schedule.md`
(~2.5 hrs/day, ~12.5 hrs/week). Rough weekly time budget:

- Instagram: 4–5 posts — batch-record once, edit/post across the week
- Facebook: near-zero extra (cross-post) + 30–45 min/week in groups
- YouTube: 1 long-form every 2 weeks (biggest single time cost) + Shorts free from the Reels
- Blog: 1 post/week
- Google Business Profile: 1 post/week + review asks

If a week gets tight, protect Instagram and Google Business Profile first —
they're the two most likely to produce a lead inside this 100-day window.
YouTube and blog can slip a few days without losing much; they're playing a
longer game either way.
