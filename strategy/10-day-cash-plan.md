## Rules for these ten days

**Ask for advances on everything.** 50% up front is standard, professional, and
the only thing that converts a signature into cash. You cannot fund other
people's projects from ₹0.

**Stop all free work.** Strategy decks, prototypes, "initial support" — every hour
spent on unpaid work this week is an hour not spent on paid work. You have
already proven you're good. Nobody in your pipeline needs more proof.

**Chase daily.** Not weekly. A signed proposal is not money. Money is money.

**Sell what you can deliver alone.** Websites and strategy need no freelancers, no
shoot day, no upfront cost. Content-heavy work needs cash you don't have.
Preference goes to what you can ship solo with AI leverage.

**Don't discount to close.** At ₹0 cash the temptation to take anything is
enormous. A cheap client you resent for six months costs more than an empty
month.

You are right that dead profiles are costing you. Every prospect checks before
they reply, and silence reads as *"this studio isn't working."* That is a trust
problem — and trust is one of the reasons you guessed you were losing deals.

Do it, but keep it in proportion: **this supports the three sales conversations
above, it does not replace them.** It brings no cash in ten days.

Post from what you already own — zero production cost:

- The OTHO strategy work — anonymised until signed
- Your own brand system — logo matrix, palette, cards, all built and never shown
- The thinking behind a decision: why one layout beat another

That is three weeks of posts sitting in this repo. Enough that anyone checking
you out on Friday or Monday sees a studio that is clearly working.
