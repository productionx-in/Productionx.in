### Post 2 — Wednesday. Pillar 4: what to ask a studio before hiring them.

> Five questions worth asking any studio before you sign, from someone who's
> sat on the hiring side of this exact decision:
>
> **1. Who actually shoots this — you, or a subcontractor I've never met?**
> Ask this directly. The answer changes who's accountable when something goes
> wrong on set.
>
> **2. What happens if the strategy and the production disagree?**
> If strategy and production are two different vendors, this is where
> projects quietly fall apart — nobody owns the disagreement.
>
> **3. How fast do you actually reply to a brief?**
> Not "we're responsive." A number. Ours is 24 hours, and we hold to it
> because a brand manager on a deadline doesn't have a week to wait for a
> "let me check."
>
> **4. What's the one round of revisions actually cover?**
> This is where scope creep starts. Get it in writing before day one, not
> after the invoice.
>
> **5. Can I see the process, not just the highlight reel?**
> Anyone can show you the best 30 seconds. Ask to see how a project actually
> runs, brief to delivery.
>
> — ProductionX, Hyderabad

### Post 3 — Friday. Pillar 4: the staging idea, generalised (from real work this month).

> A client asked us for a full platform — listings, search, an admin back
> office, user accounts, the works. Real scope, real budget, eventually.
>
> The instinct when a budget doesn't match a big ask is to shrink the price.
> We didn't do that. We shrunk what the first phase includes instead.
>
> Phase one: what gets them live and taking real enquiries, priced properly,
> six weeks. Phase two: everything else, quoted once phase one is running and
> the real usage data exists to scope it accurately.
>
> Same rate. Smaller, honest first bite. And a client who isn't stuck
> guessing what a "full platform" actually costs before they've seen a single
> screen.
>
> If a quote feels too big to say yes to, the fix usually isn't a discount.
> It's a smaller first phase at the real price.
>
> — ProductionX, Hyderabad
