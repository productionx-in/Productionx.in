## LinkedIn — 3 posts

### Post 1 — Monday. The five-step process, made concrete.

> Most studios describe their process in one vague line: "we handle
> everything." Here's what that actually means at ProductionX, step by step:
>
> **Brief & discovery** — a real 30-minute call, not a form. We're trying to
> understand the business, not just the brief.
>
> **Strategy & planning** — competitor study, positioning, a real calendar
> with shoot dates before anything gets filmed.
>
> **Production** — our crew, our kit, or the render pipeline when that's the
> faster route. Nothing subcontracted out to someone we've never worked with.
>
> **Edit & review** — one structured round of feedback. You see cuts, not a
> black box you wait on.
>
> **Delivery** — on schedule, with a report behind it, not just a folder of
> files and a goodbye.
>
> Ask any studio you're evaluating to walk you through their version of this.
> If they can't, that's the answer.
>
> — ProductionX, Hyderabad

### Post 2 — Wednesday. The 24-hour reply rule, and why it exists.

> We reply to every brief within 24 hours. Not because it's a nice policy —
> because we've been on the other end of a brief that sat in someone's inbox
> for a week while a launch date got closer.
>
> A brand manager on a deadline doesn't need a perfect answer immediately.
> They need to know someone's actually looking at it.
>
> If a studio takes a week to reply to a first message, that's not a one-time
> thing. That's how the whole engagement runs.
>
> — ProductionX, Hyderabad

### Post 3 — Friday. What "one round of revisions" should actually mean.

> Scope creep doesn't start on day 30 of a project. It starts in the line of
> the contract nobody reads carefully: "revisions included."
>
> One round should mean something specific and written down — not a vague
> promise that turns into six rounds because nobody defined the boundary.
>
> Before you sign anything: ask what counts as a revision, what counts as a
> new request, and get the answer in writing. It protects both sides, and any
> studio that's uncomfortable writing it down is telling you something.
>
> — ProductionX, Hyderabad
