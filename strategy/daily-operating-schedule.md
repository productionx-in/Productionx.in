# The daily 10 hours

One person, one day, two jobs: keep the business alive this month, and build the
thing that means you're not in this position again. Both need protected time —
if content only gets "whatever's left," it gets nothing, because there is always
a client fire that feels more urgent.

**The split: ~6 hours revenue work, ~2.5 hours content (you as the face), ~1.5
hours admin/planning.** Revenue leads because the bank account says so. Content
still gets a fixed block, every day, non-negotiable — it's the thing that gets
you out of this cycle, and six weeks from now it starts paying rent.

---

## The default day

| Time | Block | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **9:00–9:30** | Plan | Yesterday's results, today's 3 must-dos. Written down, not in your head. |
| **9:30–12:30** | **Revenue — deep work** | Live client deliverables. Right now: finish Mahati/Aruna to invoiceable. Once that's shipped, this slot becomes OTHO Phase 1 prep and prototype polish. |
| **12:30–1:15** | Break | Actually stop. |
| **1:15–2:45** | **Revenue — outreach & sales** | Warm-network conversations, follow-ups, new prospecting, proposal writing. This is where new deals get found — it doesn't happen by itself. |
| **2:45–3:00** | Break | |
| **3:00–5:30** | **Content — you as the face** | Record, write, film. See pillars below. This is the block that's easiest to skip and most important not to. |
| **5:30–6:30** | **Content — publish & engage** | Post, reply to comments/DMs, edit tomorrow's piece. Engagement is not optional — it's half of why founder-led content works. |
| **6:30–7:00** | Admin | Invoices, chasing payment, inbox, tomorrow's plan. |

Shift the blocks to fit your actual hours — the ratio is what matters, not the
clock times.

## Why this split and not something else

**Revenue is ~60% because you have no cushion.** Content compounds, but it
compounds over months — the original strategy expected the first inbound lead
around week 6–8. You don't have 6–8 weeks of runway sitting idle. Every deal in
motion (Mahati, OTHO, the next prospect) needs deep work and needs chasing, or
it drifts the way OTHO's timeline already has.

**Content is protected, not optional, because it's the only channel that isn't
waiting on someone else's decision.** OTHO waits on their budget. Prospecting
waits on replies. Content is entirely in your control, and it's the one thing
building an asset instead of just paying this month's bill — it's also your
hedge against being the single point of failure in the business a year from now.

**As cash stabilises, the ratio moves.** Once OTHO and Mahati land, dial revenue
down to ~5 hours and content up to ~3.5. Right now, survival first.

## Content — you as the face

You already said you'll be the face. That's the right call: it's the
differentiator competitors with bigger teams can't copy, and it's what the
original strategy flagged as the single highest-ROI channel currently unused.

**Two platforms, different jobs:**

- **LinkedIn — 3×/week.** This reaches the buyer directly: marketing heads,
  developers, business owners. Not "we delivered X for client Y." Write what
  only someone who's actually run these projects can write — what a real-estate
  founder should ask before hiring a studio, why a ₹1.5L quote and an ₹80k budget
  aren't actually the same negotiation, what a Phase 1 / Phase 2 split protects
  against. Thinking, not portfolio.
- **Instagram — 4–5×/week to start** (daily once the batch-content habit is
  built). Process, not just polish: you building, deciding, fixing. Talking-head
  clips work here better than produced reels right now — faster to make, and
  "founder explains" content is what builds trust before a shoot budget exists.

**Four pillars, so you're never stuck on what to post:**

1. **Live process** — the Mahati/Aruna build, OTHO's strategy thinking
   (anonymised until signed), whatever's on your desk this week
2. **Point of view** — the LinkedIn thought-leadership above
3. **Spec teardowns** — publicly rebuild or critique a real Hyderabad
   business's marketing. Zero permission needed, zero cost, and it's exactly
   the kind of content that already brought you one AI-platform lead
4. **Founder story** — why AI-leveraged, why real estate, what you're building
   and why "the face" is you specifically

**Zero-cost assets already sitting in this repo** to seed the first two weeks:
the Aruna/Mahati site, the OTHO prototype and strategy deck, the unused logo
matrix, palette and business cards in `brand/`. That's easily ten posts before
you have to originate anything new.

## What this week actually needs, inside the schedule

- **Revenue block, days 1–2:** finish Mahati/Aruna, invoice on delivery
- **Revenue block, ongoing:** warm-network outreach, keep OTHO warm without
  giving away more free work, prospect new accounts
- **Content block, day 1:** batch-record 3–4 talking-head clips in one sitting —
  this is how "daily" content survives a busy revenue week, you're not filming
  fresh every single day
- **Content block, ongoing:** 3 LinkedIn posts, 4–5 Instagram posts, replies to
  anyone who engages

---

## One rule

If a day gets eaten entirely by a client fire, the content block moves — it
doesn't disappear. Reschedule it into the next open slot, don't let "no time
today" become "no time this week."
