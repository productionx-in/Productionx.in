# Designerz Hub — the ₹80k package

**Call this in tomorrow (Fri 21 Aug).** They are the warmest money in the pipeline:
they know the work, they named a budget, and they never said no to ProductionX —
they said no to ₹1.5L.

---

## First: check one thing before you call

Their "₹80k" is ambiguous and it changes the whole package.

| If ₹80k means | Then your fee is | Package to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ₹80k/month **fee**, ad spend on top | ₹80k | **Package A** below |
| ₹80k/month **all-in** incl. ad spend | ~₹55k fee + ₹25k media | **Package B** below |
| ₹80k **one-time** project | ₹80k | **Package C** below |

Ask it plainly in the first two minutes: *"When you said ₹80k — was that the
monthly fee with ad budget separate, or everything included?"* Do not guess and
do not build a proposal on the wrong reading.

---

## The rule that matters more than the package

**Do not discount ₹1.5L down to ₹80k. Cut scope, keep your rate.**

If you deliver ₹1.5L of work for ₹80k, three things happen: you lose money on
every hour, you resent the client by month two, and you can never raise the
price because you've proven you'll do it for less. A discount also tells them
your first number was invented.

The honest line, and you can say it exactly like this:

> "₹1.5L was the right price for everything. It's not the right price for ₹80k —
> so I'm not going to give you the same thing cheaper. I've built you a smaller
> plan that does one job properly instead of four jobs badly."

That sentence protects your pricing *and* makes you look more professional than
the agency that would have just dropped to ₹80k.

## Package A — ₹80k/month fee, ad spend separate

### "Showroom Demand Engine"

Furniture is a **considered, high-ticket, visual, local** purchase. Nobody buys a
₹90,000 sofa off a Reel. What actually moves their revenue is **qualified people
walking into the showroom** — so that is the only number the package promises.

**In scope**
- **Meta ads only** (Instagram + Facebook) — furniture is discovery-led and visual;
  this is where the category performs
- **One shoot day per month**, batched — 10–12 pieces of content produced in a
  single visit instead of continuous production. This is the cost control that
  makes ₹80k possible.
- **3 posts/week** from that batch
- **WhatsApp enquiry routing** — every ad click lands in a WhatsApp conversation,
  not a dead form
- **Monthly report on enquiries and showroom visits** — not reach, not followers

**Explicitly NOT in scope** — say this out loud, it is what makes ₹80k credible:
- Google Ads *(add-on: ₹35k/mo — recommended once Meta proves out)*
- Daily posting
- Content beyond the monthly batch
- Influencer / UGC campaigns
- Website work

**Ad spend:** ₹30–50k/month, paid by them directly to Meta. Not your money, not
your margin, never routed through you.

## Package B — ₹80k/month all-inclusive

Same as A, but: ₹55k fee + ₹25k ad spend, and content drops to 6–8 pieces from
the monthly batch.

**Be straight about the trade-off:** ₹25k/month of Meta spend in Hyderabad
furniture is a small test budget. It will generate learning and some enquiries,
but not scale. Tell them that up front — if it underperforms in month two, you
want it on record that spend was the constraint, not your work.

Recommend Package A. Offer B only if they insist on one all-in number.

## Package C — ₹80k one-time project

If they want a project, not a retainer: **"Brand & Launch Kit"** — 4 weeks.

Brand refresh (you already own the tooling), one production day, 20 assets, Meta
ad account set up properly with pixel and catalogue, 4 weeks of ads managed, and
a 90-day playbook they could run themselves.

Ends with the natural question: *"Do you want to keep running this, or should we?"*
That is your retainer conversation, already earned.

## The ladder — show them this, it is the real point

Put all three tiers in the proposal. The ₹80k stops looking like a discount and
starts looking like the entry rung of a real business.

| Tier | ₹/month | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Showroom Demand Engine** | 80,000 | Meta + batched content. One channel, done properly. |
| **Growth** | 1,20,000 | Adds Google Ads + weekly content |
| **Full Marketing Partner** | 1,50,000 | Everything you originally quoted |

*"Most clients start at tier one and move up in month three, once the numbers
justify it."* — that is true, and it makes ₹80k a beginning instead of a ceiling.

## Payment terms — this is the part that pays your dues

Your cash problem is not the monthly value. It is **when the first rupee arrives.**

- **50% advance on signing — ₹40,000**
- Balance on day 30
- Then monthly in advance
- 3-month minimum *(furniture has a long consideration cycle; one month proves
  nothing and you will get blamed for it)*

**₹40,000 in hand this week is the entire purpose of this call.** Do not let the
conversation end with "send me the proposal and I'll look at it." End it with a
signing date.

## What to actually say tomorrow

**Opening — do not apologise, do not re-pitch:**

> "I've been thinking about our last conversation. You were right — ₹1.5L was
> more than you needed to spend right now. I've built something at your number
> that I actually believe in. Ten minutes?"

**Then the budget clarification.** Then the package. Then:

**The close:**
> "If this works for you, I'll send the agreement today. We'd shoot in the first
> week and have ads live by the end of the month."

**If they push below ₹80k:** they named ₹80k themselves. Hold it. Instead of
cutting price, cut scope again — drop to 2 posts/week, or a 6-week pilot at ₹55k.
Never the same work for less money.

**If they stall:** *"What would you need to see to decide by Friday?"* Then give
them exactly that, and nothing more.
